# melzayathub

Centralized dashboard, projects, and initiatives hub.

## Projects

### 1. Agent Cost Dashboard
Real-time tracking of daily and monthly costs across all agents (Brainy, Striker, Opsy, Browsy).
- **URL**: `/projects/agent-cost-dashboard`
- **Features**: Daily cost tracking, monthly breakdown, trend analysis, cost entry form
- **Data**: Test data with Chart.js visualizations

### 2. أنا جاهز (I Am Ready)
Egyptian graduate training initiative targeting 1 million graduates across 27 governorates.
- **URL**: `/projects/ana-gahiz`
- **Focus**: Presidential initiative, 5 Cisco-certified training tracks, 420+ partner institutions
- **Timeline**: 2026-2030, $150M USD budget

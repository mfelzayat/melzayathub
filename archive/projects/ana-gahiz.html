Project Overview

أنا جاهز (I Am Ready) is a national Egyptian initiative to train and empower 1 million graduates across 27 governorates with Cisco-certified technical skills. The program targets 200,000 graduates annually over a 5-year period (2026-2030) with a $150 million USD budget.
